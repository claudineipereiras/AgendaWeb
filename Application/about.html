Our Mission

An Open-Web Cultural Broadsheet for Live Music & Independent Stages

AgendaWeb is an open-web, community-first cultural publication and decentralized gig directory designed specifically for Australia’s independent venues, artist-run spaces, and local audiences.

Mainstream social algorithms increasingly hide live music behind paywalls, walled gardens, and opaque feeds. AgendaWeb returns to the open principles of the World Wide Web: direct RSS syndication, machine-readable microformats (Schema.org JSON-LD), standard iCalendar feeds, and private client-side storage with zero user tracking.

Key Architectural Pillars

  • Direct from Source: Schedules are gathered straight from venue websites, zine feeds, and official publishers. Venues maintain sovereign ownership of their listings.
  • Privacy by Architecture: User bookmarks, followed lineups, custom RSS subscriptions, and reading queues live in your local browser’s IndexedDB. We do not track user behavior or sell data.
  • Agent & AI Ready: Full support for LLM consumption via llms.txt, clean Markdown content negotiation, RFC 8288 link discovery, and WebMCP tooling.
  • Open Standards: Native support for RSS 2.0 / Atom (feed.php) and standard iCalendar (calendar.php).
