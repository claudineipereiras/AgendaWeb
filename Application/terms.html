Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Privacy by Design (Client-Side Storage)

AgendaWeb is committed to user privacy. We do not use third-party behavioral analytics, fingerprinting, or tracking cookies. All user customizations—including followed venues, reading lists, custom RSS feeds, and theme preferences—are stored strictly on your local device using standard browser IndexedDB and LocalStorage APIs.

2. Artificial Intelligence & Automated Agents Policy

In accordance with our robots.txt and Content Signals declaration (Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no):

  • Search & Retrieval: Automated AI agents, search engines, and real-time retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) agents are welcomed to index and search cultural gig data.
  • Model Training: Bulk harvesting of original articles or broadsheet content for model pre-training or fine-tuning without explicit authorization is not permitted.
  • Machine-Readable Formats: Agents are encouraged to use our official endpoints: /llms.txt, Markdown content negotiation via Accept: text/markdown, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) server card at /.well-known/mcp/server-card.json.

3. Syndicated Content & Venue Listings

Gig schedules, concert dates, and venue information are syndicated directly from public publisher websites and official venue feeds under fair-dealing cultural indexing principles. Intellectual property of event posters, artist images, and performance descriptions remains with the respective artists and venues.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For takedown requests, feed additions, or privacy questions, please email our editorial desk directly at curator@agendaweb.app.
