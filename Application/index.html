About AgendaWeb

What is AgendaWeb?
Version 0.5.0

AgendaWeb is an independent, community-first cultural guide celebrating Melbourne’s live music, neighborhood arts, zine culture, and grassroots events.

🌱
Free & Clean Broadsheet: No algorithms, corporate paywalls, or ticket markups. We believe finding a great gig or art show should be as simple and enjoyable as picking up a neighborhood printed broadsheet.
🔒
Your Privacy Comes First: No accounts, passwords, or tracking cookies. Your saved reads, personal diary archive, and followed venues are kept 100% private in your own device.
📻
Direct from Local Venues: Gigs and reviews are gathered straight from venue websites and public broadcasters, keeping artists and independent stages in control of their own information.
