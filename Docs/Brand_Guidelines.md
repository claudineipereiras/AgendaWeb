# AgendaWeb Brand Guidelines

| Name | Hex | RGB | HSL | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Amber Rust** | `#C85A32` | `200, 90, 50` | `16°, 60%, 49%` | Brand bookmark ribbon, primary CTA buttons |

## Typography
- **Headings & Brand Title:** `Newsreader` or `Playfair Display` (Serif, weights 700/800)
- **UI Elements, Schedules & Body:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Sans-serif, weights 400/500/600/800)

## Logo Geometry & Iconography
The unified mark consists of:
1. **Bookmark Ribbon:** Solid `#C85A32` vertical badge with a square (`90°`) top-left corner, a rounded top-right shoulder, and a clean 'V' cut base (no bottom stripe line).
2. **RSS Wave Arcs:** Two rounded concentric arcs in `#4A5D4E` radiating from the top-right rounded corner.
3. **Calendar Face:** Centered `#F7F5F0` plate with two `#1B3B2B` binder rings at top.
4. **Grid Pattern:** 7 asymmetrical blocks in `#1B3B2B` arranged across 3 columns (left: 2, center: 3, right: 2).
